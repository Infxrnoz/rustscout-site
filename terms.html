The software

RustScout is a Windows companion app for the game Rust. The core app is free. RustScout Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks extra features in the app with a licence key.

RustScout is a fan-made project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Facepunch Studios. Rust and Rust+ are trademarks of Facepunch Studios. Features that depend on Rust+ only work on servers that have Rust+ working, and can stop working if Facepunch changes Rust+.

Your Pro licence

  • A Pro key is for one person and works on one PC at a time. You can move it to another PC with Remove key in the app (a few times a month).
  • Don't share, resell or publish your key. Keys that are shared may be disabled.
  • Pro unlocks features in the version of RustScout you have and in future updates. We can't promise any particular feature will keep working forever, since Rust and Windows change.

Refunds

If Pro doesn't work for you, message us within 14 days of buying and we'll refund you in full. Your key stops working after a refund.

Using it fairly

RustScout doesn't read or change the game's memory or files. Some community servers have their own rules about overlays, crosshairs or colour changes. Following the rules of the servers you play on is up to you.

Privacy

  • This website has no accounts, cookies or tracking. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.
  • The app keeps your data on your own PC: paired servers, settings, alerts, pins and your Steam and Discord settings. It talks to Rust+ (Facepunch), Steam and, if you set them up, RustMaps and Discord, to show you the information you asked for.
  • Activating Pro sends our licence server a code made from your key and an anonymous ID for your PC. That's all it stores, so it can keep a key to one PC. It doesn't see your name, files or game data.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We get your email address and the payment status from Stripe, not your card details.
  • Want something deleted? Message us and we'll remove it.

Bans

You use RustScout at your own risk. We're not responsible if you get banned, kicked or punished by a game server, Facepunch, Easy Anti-Cheat or anyone else, and we don't refund Pro because of a ban. Check the rules of the servers you play on before using the overlay, crosshair or colour profiles.

No warranty

RustScout is provided as it is. We're not responsible for lost raids, bases, items or accounts. Our total responsibility for a purchase is limited to what you paid for it.
