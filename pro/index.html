01 · Killer intel

Know who you're fighting.

Free shows who killed you. Pro pulls their public Steam profile into a card, so you can spot the cheater, the alt account or the 6,000-hour zerg player before you go back for your loot.

  • Account age, so you can spot brand-new alt accounts
  • Total Rust hours, plus the last two weeks
  • Past names, friend count and VAC or game bans
  • Live status: in Rust, on Steam or offline

Players with private Steam profiles show what Steam allows. Screenshot uses example players.

02 · Display

Stretched 4:3 in one click.

Resolution, refresh rate, HDR and scaling for every monitor, in one tab. No digging through Windows settings or graphics drivers mid-wipe.

  • 1440×1080, 1280×960 and 1024×768 presets, stretched to fill the screen
  • Every refresh rate your monitor supports, one click each
  • Any custom resolution, plus HDR on or off
  • 15-second safety: if you don't click Keep, it switches back
03 · Colour profiles

See more at night.

NVIDIA digital vibrance, saturation, brightness, contrast, gamma and temperature, saved as your own profiles. One hotkey switches between them in game, and everything goes back to normal when you close the app.

  • Night, Snow and Desert presets to start from
  • As many of your own profiles as you want
  • Ctrl + Alt + V cycles through them in game
04 · In game

Your crosshair. Your HUD.

Design a crosshair that's actually visible on snow and in the dark, and pin a small HUD over the game with the clock, time to sunset, pop, your next decay timer and live alerts.

  • Cross, T, circle or dot, with any size, gap, thickness and colour
  • Click-through, so it never steals your mouse or keyboard
  • Ctrl + Alt + M puts the whole app over the game and back

Works with Rust in Borderless or Windowed mode. Some servers don't allow crosshair overlays, so check their rules.

05 · Raid planning

Know the sulfur before you go.

Add walls, doors and deployables and see the cheapest or meta way through, with total sulfur and time to break. Your team can ask from team chat too.

  • !raid stone wall 3 and !craft right in team chat
  • !decay 250 pins a countdown to when a wall falls

Questions

Pro, explained

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time payment for a licence key, and future updates are included.

How do I get my key?

Your key shows up on screen right after you pay. In RustScout, go to Settings → RustScout Pro, paste it and click Activate.

How many PCs can I use it on?

One at a time. Got a new PC? Click Remove key on the old one, then activate it on the new one.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It needs internet once to activate, then Pro keeps working offline.

Can I get banned?

RustScout doesn't read or change the game, but some servers have rules about overlays, crosshairs or colour changes. You use it at your own risk. See the terms.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Open a ticket in our Discord within 14 days of buying for a full refund.
